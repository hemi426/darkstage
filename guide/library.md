# Library

The Library tab is your master list of songs — built up once, then reused across every show you create. A song only needs to be entered here once; adding it to a setlist just references it, so editing it later updates it everywhere.

## Adding a Song

Tap **+** to create a new song. Fields available:

- **Title** (required)
- **Artist**
- **BPM** — a number, plus a free-text field for anything a plain number can't capture ("feels rushed on the bridge," etc.)
- **Key** — pick from the chromatic picker (with major/minor/modal toggle), plus a free-text override for anything unusual
- **Time Signature** — free text (e.g. "4/4", "7/8")
- **Resource Link** — an optional link to anything outside the app: a chord chart, a lyrics page, whatever else is useful to have on hand. Tap the link icon next to the field to open it directly.
- **Notes** — the big one; see below

Every field except Title is optional — fill in only what's actually useful for a given song.

## Notes and Markdown

The Notes field supports basic markdown formatting, rendered when you're actually performing:

- `**bold**` for things that need to stand out
- `*italic*` for secondary info
- `` `monospace` `` for tabs, chord grids, or anything that needs fixed-width spacing

Tap the eye icon next to the Notes label to toggle between typing raw markdown and seeing it rendered the way it'll actually look on stage.

## The Chord/Key Picker Toolbar

When you're typing in a Key, Key Notes, or Notes field, a row of note buttons appears above your keyboard — tap a root note (C, D, E, and so on) to insert it at your cursor.

**Sharps and flats** appear as two stacked buttons rather than a toggle — tap whichever spelling you want (C♯ or D♭, for instance). A mis-tap between the two just gives you the *other* valid name for the same note, not a wrong one.

**Long-press** any root to open a popup of common suffixes (m, maj7, 7, sus2, sus4, dim, aug, 6, 9, add9, and more) — picking one inserts the root and suffix together (e.g. "Cm7").

The **Enhanced Chord Picker** (on by default — toggle it off in Settings if you'd rather not) also adds space, dash, and slash keys, for typing out chord sequences ("G - D - Em - C") or slash chords ("Gm/C") without leaving the picker.

## Markdown Formatting Toolbar

On the Notes field specifically, a second small toolbar gives you Bold, Italic, and Monospace buttons. Select some text first and tap a button to wrap it in that formatting, or tap with nothing selected to insert an empty pair of markers and start typing between them.

## Searching and Sorting

As your Library grows, use the search bar to filter by title or artist, and sort alphabetically by either — ascending or descending. Songs are grouped under sticky letter headers as you scroll, so you can jump straight to the part of the alphabet you need.
