# Performance

This is the screen you'll actually be looking at on stage. Song and talk info will be presented full-screen, high-contrast, and built to be readable and navigable without breaking your focus on playing.

## Starting a Performance

From a show's detail pane, tap the **Play** button to start a real performance session, or **Demo** to preview the show without it counting against anything (see [Demo Mode](#demo-mode) below). You can also start a performance from the Performance tab's show picker.

## Reading the Screen

Each song or talk block shows:

- **Set name**, in small caps at the top
- A **position indicator** top right (e.g. "2 / 4") showing where you are within the current set — talk blocks don't count toward or show this
- **Song title**, artist, and a row of BPM / Key / Time Signature
- A link icon next to that row if the song has a Resource Link set — tap it to open the linked chord chart, lyrics page, or whatever else you saved
- **Notes** — your song's global notes, followed by anything you added specifically for this show
- A subtle preview of the **next** song or talk block, bottom right
- Subtle **D E M O** text at the bottom left if you are in demo mode

Talk/Intro blocks show a mic icon, the block's name, and its description instead of song details.

## Gesture Controls

- **Swipe left** — advance to the next song or talk block
- **Swipe right** — go back to the previous one
- **Pinch anywhere on screen** — resize the notes text larger or smaller
- **Drag up/down along the right edge of the screen** — adjust screen brightness

The first time you enter Performance mode (or Demo mode, whichever comes first), a one-time overlay walks you through these gestures directly on the real screen layout.

## Exiting

Press your device's **Back** button or use the back gesture to bring up an Exit Performance confirmation. If you exit this way mid-show, your position is saved — coming back to the same show picks up right where you left off. Finishing a show naturally (swiping past "That's a wrap!") resets that position, so your next run-through starts fresh from the top.

## Set Breaks

Between sets, you'll see a Set Break card with a running timer. If you gave the set a target break duration back in Shows, you'll also see a countdown — the display turns red if you go over. Swipe when you're ready to continue into the next set.

## Demo Mode

Demo mode runs through a show automatically (about every 3 seconds per card, or swipe manually to override), with a small "DEMO" watermark in the corner. It's meant for previewing a show or trying out the app risk-free — it never counts against your session limit and doesn't save your position.

## Settings That Affect This Screen

A handful of settings change what you see and how brightness behaves during a performance. These settings are covered in full on the [Settings](settings.md) page, but here's a few major ones worth knowing about:

- **Auto-fit Notes** — automatically shrinks your notes text just enough to keep everything on screen, without you having to manually pinch it down
- **Full Bright by Default** — forces your screen to maximum brightness the moment you start a performance
- **Song Title Line Limit** / **'Next' Line Limit** — cap how many lines an unusually long title or next-song preview can take up before it's cut off with "…"
